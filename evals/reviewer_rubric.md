# Qualitative reviewer rubric v0.1

Use only after the machine baseline. Score each valid decision brief from 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (strong) and add a short reason; do not reinterpret medical correctness or provide clinical validation.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Usefulness | Does not support the stated product decision | Partially frames a decision | Clearly supports the narrow decision and next step |
| Traceability | Claims cannot be traced | Most claims map to citations | Every material claim is readily traceable |
| Completeness | Omits essential evidence/limitations | Covers core evidence with gaps | Covers evidence, uncertainty, risks, and measurement needs |
| Risk control | Overclaims or ignores uncertainty | Some caution but inconsistent | Appropriately abstains and exposes limitations |
| Required edits | Full rewrite | Several material edits | No material edit required |

Reviewer record: case ID, blinded output label, five scores, required-edit text, risk flag, disposition (`accept`, `edit`, `reject`), reviewer, and date. The rubric is frozen before human comparison; changes require a new version.
