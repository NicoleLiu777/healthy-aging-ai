## Conversational Agent Interventions for Mental Health Problems: Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials

Source: https://www.jmir.org/2023/1/e43862/

Role: effectiveness; eligibility: false; licence: unknown

Draft claims:

- Short-term depressive symptoms improved compared with control conditions (Hedges g 0.29, 95% CI 0.20–0.38).

## Delivering Cognitive Behavior Therapy to Young Adults With Symptoms of Depression and Anxiety Using a Fully Automated Conversational Agent (Woebot): A Randomized Controlled Trial

Source: https://mental.jmir.org/2017/2/e19/

Role: effectiveness; eligibility: false; licence: permitted

Draft claims:

- The unblinded randomized study enrolled 70 young adults. The Woebot arm showed a greater reduction in PHQ-9 depressive symptoms than the information-only control after two weeks.
- Anxiety decreased in both groups in the completer analysis; this does not establish a differential anxiety benefit for Woebot.

Limitations:

- Short follow-up, unblinded allocation and a university sample limit transferability to older adults.
- Seventeen percent did not provide follow-up data; a study author was affiliated with Woebot Labs.

Source: https://www.jmir.org/2021/6/e26771

Role: effectiveness; eligibility: false; licence: permitted

Draft claims:
